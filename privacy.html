Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This policy explains how games made by Spearhill AB (organisation number 559496-3133, Sweden) handle your information.

What our games collect

  • No personal data. Our games have no accounts of their own, no analytics, no telemetry and no advertising. They do not send information about you or your play to us or to anyone else.
  • Saves and settings stay on your device. Progress, settings and log files are stored only on your own device. Log files exist to help you report a problem, and are shared only if you choose to send them to us.
  • Stores and platforms. When you buy or play through a store such as Steam or Google Play, that store handles your purchase, your account and any platform features (such as achievements) under its own privacy policy. We receive no personal information from stores beyond what they provide to all developers, such as aggregated sales figures.
  • Playing together online. Where a game offers online play, it connects directly to the player hosting the session. As with any direct online connection, the players' devices exchange network addresses to connect. Nothing passes through servers of ours.

If a game ever handles data differently, this page will say so, for that game, before it is released.

Children

Our games do not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Contacting us

If you email us, Spearhill AB uses your email address and what you write to handle your message (legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering you). Our email is provided by Google (Google Workspace), which processes it on our behalf. We keep messages for as long as needed to deal with them, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

You have the right to request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing. Contact us at the address below. You can also complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
